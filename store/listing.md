## English (en-US)

**Title:** Zombie Deck

**Short description:** Unofficial zombie spawn deck for Zombicide 2nd Edition. Draw, shuffle, survive!

**Full description:**

Zombie Deck replaces the zombie spawn deck of Zombicide 2nd Edition on your phone or tablet. No more shuffling a big pile of cards between turns: tap the deck, read the card, place your zombies.

• Every spawn card of the core box, with the zombie count for your Danger Level
• Rush, Extra Activation and Abomination cards, just like the real ones
• Choose the Danger Level in one tap; show only the current level or all four
• Shuffle the draw pile or reshuffle everything, and undo a draw
• Your game is saved automatically: close the app and pick up where you left off
• The screen stays on during the game
• Works offline, no ads, no account, no data collected

Expansions and promos (switch them on in the Expansions & promos menu):
Fort Hendrix, Toxic City Mall, Prison Outbreak, Rue Morgue, Angry Neighbors, Zombie Dogz, Murder of Crowz, VIP: Very Infected People, Lost Zombivors, the extra Abomination cards, Danny Trejo and Monty Python's Flying Circus.

Available in English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese and Russian, with the official zombie names of each edition.

You need your own copy of the game to play: the app only replaces the spawn deck.

Fan-made and unofficial. Zombicide is a trademark of CMON Global Limited; this app is not affiliated with or endorsed by CMON.
