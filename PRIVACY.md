# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 3, 2026_

Zombie Deck does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- It does not use the network: everything works offline.
- Your settings (language, card display, expansions) and your current game are saved only on your device, so you can resume a game. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Navis62/zombideck/issues
