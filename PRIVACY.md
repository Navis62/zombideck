# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 4, 2026_

Zombie Deck does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- Everything works offline. When a connection is available, the app checks Expo's update service (expo.dev) for corrections to the cards or translations and downloads them. This request carries only technical information needed to deliver the right update (platform, app version), no personal data.
- Your settings (language, card display, vibration, expansions), your current game in each universe and the number of cards drawn (used to ask once for a store rating) are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- The rating prompt is shown by Google Play or the App Store; the app does not see your rating.
- No data is sent to the developer.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Navis62/zombideck/issues
